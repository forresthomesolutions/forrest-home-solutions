Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Tell us about your project using the estimate form, or call to discuss the details.

What areas do you serve?

Based in Sarasota, we serve nearby communities throughout Southwest Florida, including Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte and surrounding areas.

How long does a painting project take?

Timing depends on the scope and preparation. A typical exterior paint job may take around 4–5 days, while a typical interior may take around 6–8 days depending on furniture and site conditions.

Can I send pictures of the work I need?

Yes. Use the estimate form to prepare an email, then attach your photos before sending.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We offer repairs, painting, turnovers, and ongoing property maintenance.
